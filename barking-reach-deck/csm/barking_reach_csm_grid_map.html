Evidence Stack
  • Digimap Historic OS: 1864 / 1895–1916 maps show Dagenham Breach, marshland and later made ground; 1939–40 maps show cable works, car-body / motor works, concrete blocks, wagon repair, pump house, jetty, crane and rail sidings.
  • Digimap Land-Line 2005: 33 mapped labels extracted — tanks, depots, power station, works, chimneys, conveyors, substations, ETL, roads and Dagenham Breach.
  • 20/01094/FULL: Remediation, below-ground demolition, cooling-water intake / outfall tunnels, pump station, gas / fuel-distillate / utility infrastructure.
  • Planning conditions: Contamination survey, water-quality monitoring, UXO, asbestos and contamination removal.
  • Lucion / Delta-Simons: RED potentially contaminated area; asbestos in soils and hydrogen-sulphide hazards identified during ground investigation.
  • EA + BGS: Historic Landfill, GeoIndex boreholes and London Earth metals background used to calibrate confidence and pollutant family.
  • OSM / Overpass: Rail, service roads, water edge, industrial buildings and current infrastructure used as spatial anchors.
