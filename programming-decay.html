Cover · Introduction

Programming DecayA Multi-Scalar Design Intelligence Framework

Concrete is the most produced material in human history — and one of the most polluting. Two centuries of industrial cement production have left a chemical sediment across the planet. Programming Decay asks a different question: not how to clean up faster, but how to design intelligence into the cleanup itself. Six chapters develop the framework — from global pollution context, UK-to-site contamination legacy, the material pathology of concrete, algorithmic site selection at Dagenham Dock, machine-learning experiments in spatial intelligence, to a final architectural proposition — the Planetary Intelligence Hub.

Chapter 01

The Global Context of PollutionGlobal Energy × Urban Emissions × Cement Economy

From energy infrastructure to urban accumulation — tracing the planetary metabolism that makes concrete possible.

Chapter 02

Contamination LegacyAtmospheric Emissions × Heavy Metal Deposition × Construction Waste

Atmospheric emissions, heavy metal deposition, and construction waste across England — converging on East London. This chapter maps the wound; the chapters that follow build the nervous system that learns to heal it.

Chapter 03

Material & RemediationHazards, Heavy Metal Network, Bio-Cycling Toolkit

The concrete production cycle as a closed loop of contamination — assembling a toolkit of biological, chemical, and mechanical remediation tactics for an intelligent infrastructure to deploy.

Chapter 04

Algorithmic Site SelectionFrom Data to Strategy at Dagenham Dock

Data-driven identification of Dagenham Dock through spatial clustering, sentiment validation, and multi-layer site reading — revealing four strategies as the spatial operating system of a regenerating territory.

Chapter 05

Human, Machine Learning and ArchitecturePix2Pix Translation × Neural Cellular Automata

Pix2Pix spatial translation + Neural Cellular Automata temporal simulation — learned rules replacing top-down prescription.

Chapter 06

Planetary Intelligence HubFour Towers, Two Bridges, One Ground

Four towers, two bridges, one ground — the Hub as the physical interface of a self-updating site model.

Appendix

Methodological ValidationNeural CA — Training Evidence and Hidden State Analysis

Training evidence, hidden states, and bibliographic record — the NCA is trained on Recypark Jette before transferring to Dagenham. Convergence on a known case validates the learning behaviour; Dagenham inherits the validated method.
