Nantou Cyber-VillageCoursework — 2024

An urban design proposal for Nantou Ancient Town, Shenzhen — a historic urban village under pressure from the surrounding metropolis. The project challenges the rigid single-household plot system by introducing a new shared-living typology: a generatively designed tower that fuses Chinese spatial traditions with a modular, collectively owned living arrangement.

深圳南头古城——一座被都市夹住的历史城中村——的城市设计提案。设计挑战僵硬的单户单宅制，提出一种新的共享居住类型：用一套生成式逻辑，把中国空间传统与模块化、可被集体使用的居住方式融合在同一座塔里。

01 — Site Analysis

Current Conditions, Site History & Demand

Nantou Ancient Town sits at the centre of Shenzhen's commercial corridors. Its site history stretches from the Song Dynasty through the Republican era to its current status as an intensely inhabited urban village. The analysis of current conditions maps overlapping problems: overcrowded drainage infrastructure, residential buildings in extremely close proximity, narrow street space, and low-rise storefronts compressed between towers. Crowd demand analysis identifies three primary user groups — photographers and tourists (15%), local residents (13%), and business visitors (43%) — whose differing needs and time patterns form the social basis of the design brief.

南头古城卡在深圳商业走廊的中心。场地史从宋代延伸到民国，再到今天作为高密度城中村的状态。现状分析叠加多重问题：超载的排水设施、贴在一起的住宅、被压缩的街巷、被塔楼夹住的低层店铺。人群需求分析把使用者拆成三类——摄影者与游客（15%）、本地居民（13%）、商业访客（43%）——他们各自的诉求与时段，构成了设计任务书的社会基础。

02 — Design Strategy

From Bounded to Boundless: Shared Living Model & Generative Process

The overall strategy contrasts two conditions: 有界 (bounded) — the existing single-household plot with no shared space — versus 无界 (boundless) — a new model where private residence coexists with open space, shared modules, and improved road connectivity. The reconstruction strategy defines unit types by household size (from 1 Adult 25㎡ to 2–4 Adults 170㎡) alongside six shared spatial components: external staircase, spiral green space, suspended balcony, Chinese decorative elements, hanging garden, and staircase green space. The building form is generated through twelve steps — from a base box through framework, shuffle, road, circulation, added units, overlap, combination, balcony, rotation, raised base, to a final form that blends tradition.

整体策略对比两种状态：有界——既有的单户单宅，没有共享空间；无界——一种新模型，把私人居所与开放空间、共享模块、改善后的路网连接整合在一起。改造策略按家庭规模定义户型（从单人 25㎡ 到 2–4 人 170㎡），并配六类共享构件：外挂楼梯、螺旋绿化、悬挑阳台、中式装饰构件、悬挂花园、楼梯绿化。形态生成分十二步——基础体块 → 框架 → 错动 → 路 → 流线 → 加户 → 叠加 → 组合 → 阳台 → 旋转 → 抬底 → 最终融合传统的形态。

03 — Spatial Layout

Detail Axonometric & Master Plan

The exploded axonometric breaks the tower into its full vertical programme: commercial units at the base, lofts and studios above, then layers of shared spaces — recreation room, gym, study room, shared balcony — interspersed with open terraces, spiral green corridors, and vertical pocket gardens, crowned by Chinese pavilion roofs. The master plan shows how multiple such towers are arranged across the site, with atria, courtyards, and shared ground-level spaces connecting them.

爆炸轴测把塔楼的纵向功能逐层拆开：底层商业、上方 loft 与工作室，再是层叠的共享空间——活动室、健身房、自习室、共享阳台——其间嵌入开放露台、螺旋绿廊与垂直口袋花园，最上方是中式檐顶。总平面则显示多座塔如何在场地中排布，由中庭、院落与共享地面空间彼此相连。

04 — Building Programme

Structural Logic & Architectural Rendering

The building is structured by four spatial systems: a vertical internal core of elevators and stairs; a public shared function zone at ground level with commercial use following crowd flow; an outer cycle of spiral green terraces and exterior stairs wrapping the perimeter to create new vertical circulation; and an open base raised off the ground to widen the street passage beneath. The building sample analysis shows how the roof profile adapts traditional Chinese pavilion eaves and the gable wall adopts Chinese lattice decoration. The street-level render shows the completed structure from eye level — layers of inhabited terraces rising above a dense urban street, capped by traditional canopies.

建筑由四个空间系统组织：一道由电梯与楼梯组成的纵向核；地面层是顺人流走的公共共享功能与商业；外圈是包裹周长的螺旋绿台与外挂楼梯，形成新的垂直流线；底部抬起一段，让街道在塔下变宽。建筑样本分析显示屋顶形廓如何继承中式檐口、山墙如何采用中式格栅。街景渲染从人眼高度回看建成后的塔——层层的居住露台浮在密集街巷之上，被传统檐顶收尾。

05 — Visualisation & Shared-Space App

Aerial Render, Interior Spaces & Resident Booking Interface

The aerial render shows the completed complex among Shenzhen's high-rises — multiple towers with cascading traditional pavilion roofs forming a new urban landmark within the village fabric. Residents manage and book shared spaces through a companion app: viewing utilisation rates, booking rooms, sharing kitchens or bathrooms, checking community activities, and receiving conservation guidance for rooftop small farms. Interior renders show the three spatial registers: Private Apartment (warm, domestic), Semi-Private Public Space (open terrace with planting), and Public Coffee Shop (ground-floor commercial).

鸟瞰渲染把建成后的塔群放回深圳高层背景——多座带有层叠檐顶的塔，在城中村肌理中形成新的地标。居民通过一款配套 App 管理与预订共享空间：查看使用率、预订房间、共享厨房或卫浴、查看社区活动、获取屋顶小型农园的养护提示。室内渲染呈现三种空间层级：私人住宅（温暖、家居）、半私密公共空间（带绿植的开放露台）、公共咖啡馆（底层商业）。
