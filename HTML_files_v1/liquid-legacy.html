Liquid LegacyUndergraduate Thesis — 2024

An LBS + AR exploration game prototype set at the post-industrial shipyard ruins of Fuxing Island, Shanghai. The design transforms a heavily contaminated waterfront into an interactive public landscape — making the sewage treatment process visible, educational, and playable.

把上海复兴岛的后工业船厂废墟，做成一座 LBS + AR 探索游戏的原型场地。设计把被污染严重的滨水带翻译成一座可交互的公共景观——让污水处理过程变得可见、可学、可玩。

01 — Site & Context

Location Analysis, History & Design Strategy

Shanghai's Fuxing Island has operated as an industrial shipyard since 1910, accumulating over a century of heavy metal and petroleum hydrocarbon contamination. The project responds to an urban renewal initiative by making remediation legible — turning the cleanup process itself into a landscape experience. The overall strategy integrates three programmatic layers: Theme Activity Park, Popular Science Education, and Sewage Purification.

复兴岛自 1910 年起作为工业船厂运转，累积了一个世纪的重金属与石油烃污染。设计回应一项城市更新计划——把"修复"做成可被阅读的景观，让清理过程本身成为景观体验。整体策略分三层：主题活动公园、科普教育、污水净化。

02 — Strategy & Program

Population Analysis, Design Framework & App Interface

Three user groups — retired elderly, families with children, and youth — inform a three-part design strategy: sewage treatment infrastructure as landscape (Part 1), interactive participation through AR and ecological restoration (Part 2), and landscape community events (Part 3). The accompanying app prototype supports activity booking, gamified progression, and event timelines. Event poster designs address Wetland Plant Observation, Water Celebration, Shipyard Theatre, and AR Interactive Exhibition.

三类用户——退休老人、亲子家庭、青年——支撑起三部分策略：景观化的污水处理基础设施（Part 1）、由 AR 和生态修复驱动的互动参与（Part 2）、社区景观活动（Part 3）。配套 App 原型支持活动预约、关卡式进阶与活动时间线。海报覆盖湿地植物观察、水的庆典、船厂剧场、AR 互动展四类活动。

03 — Spatial Design

Shape Generation, Physical Model & Master Plan

Building form is generated by extracting circuit board patterns and adapting them through five steps: Original → Transform → Split → Assembly → Join. The 1:300 physical model (900×600mm, basswood board, acrylic and resin) demonstrates the result. Five functional nodes — Water Treatment Path, Boarding Point, Science Exhibition Centre, Visitor & Interaction Centre, and Parkland — are arranged along the master plan.

建筑形态从电路板纹样中抽取，分五步演化：Original → Transform → Split → Assembly → Join。1:300 实体模型（900×600mm，椴木板、亚克力与树脂）呈现最终结果。总平面上布置五个功能节点：水处理路径、登船点、科普展示中心、游客与互动中心、公园用地。

04 — Water System

Sewage Treatment as Landscape

The complete wastewater treatment sequence — coarse filtration → primary sedimentation → biological treatment → secondary sedimentation → disinfection → constructed wetlands — is embedded into the public landscape. The profile section shows visitors moving from 0% to 95% purification along the treatment path, with each stage corresponding to a distinct spatial experience from the Warehouse Area at 0% purification through to the Huangpu River Basin.

完整污水处理流程——粗格栅 → 初沉 → 生物处理 → 二沉 → 消毒 → 人工湿地——被嵌入公共景观。剖面显示访客沿处理路径从 0% 推进到 95% 的净化度，每一阶段对应一种不同的空间体验：从 0% 净化的仓储区，一直走到黄浦江流域。

05 — AR Interaction & Visualization

Three AR Scenes, Landscape Render & Exploded View

Three AR interactive scenes: (1) Microbial activated sludge animation — observing pollutant decomposition in real time; (2) Second sedimentation tank purification — witnessing the transition from turbid to clear; (3) Microscopic observation of microorganisms — discovering ciliates, rotifers and other communities in the sludge. The aerial landscape visualization and exploded construction diagram complete the project.

三套 AR 交互场景：(1) 活性污泥微生物动画——实时观察污染物降解；(2) 二沉池净化——见证从浑浊到清澈的过程；(3) 微生物镜下观察——发现纤毛虫、轮虫等污泥中的微生物群落。鸟瞰景观渲染与爆炸构造图作为项目的收束。
