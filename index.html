01.A
Embedded Demo · Programming Decay V3 · Entropy Atlas included

Operating the decision machine in place

Open standalone ↗

From planetary cement metabolism down to a single 8×8 cell at Dagenham Dock, the thesis is compressed into clickable product logic. Every scroll, every layer toggle, every inference call is one round of reasoning over the same contaminated ground. The black box is unfolded into explainable steps; the viewer is asked to drop the looking-at-a-project posture and enter an operating-a-system posture, where the decision is no longer the designer's alone but a result of human–machine collaboration.

The Entropy Atlas — the n8n-orchestrated evidence layer of ~1,000 East-London post-industrial sites — is included inside this demo as the site-evidence layer.

从全球水泥代谢，到 Dagenham Dock 上的某一格 8×8 网格，毕设研究被压缩成可点击的产品逻辑。每一次滚动、每一次切换图层、每一次跑推理，都是对同一片污染场地的一次再判断。黑箱被翻译成可被解释的步骤；观者被邀请放下看作品的姿态，进入用系统的姿态——决策不再属于设计师一个人，而是人与机器协同的结果。

熵图集（Entropy Atlas）—— 由 n8n 自动化采集的东伦敦约 1,000 处后工业场地证据层 —— 已作为场地证据嵌入在该 Demo 内部。

04   TRAJECTORY WHY THE TRANSITION IS COHERENT

About关于

I am a Master's student at UCL Bartlett RC18 (Urban Design), with a Landscape Architecture undergraduate degree from Zhejiang Wanli University. My current thesis, Programming Decay, develops a multi-scalar decision framework for fungal bioremediation of contaminated post-industrial concrete at Dagenham Dock, East London.

The work I care about sits where spatial intelligence, decision systems, and AI-assisted workflows meet. Architecture trained me to hold input, constraint, stakeholder, and output in the same diagram — the same skill that AI Product Management needs.

I am moving into AI Product Management / AI Product Design where this systems thinking can be applied directly to geospatial interfaces, decision-support tools, and human–AI workflows.

UCL Bartlett RC18 城市设计在读硕士，本科为浙江万里学院风景园林。在读毕设《Programming Decay》在伦敦东 Dagenham Dock 建立一套多尺度决策框架，用于污染后工业混凝土的真菌生物修复。

关心的工作落在空间智能、决策系统与 AI 辅助工作流的交叉点。建筑训练让我能把输入、约束、相关方与输出收进同一张图——这正是 AI 产品所需要的能力。

正在向 AI 产品经理 / AI 产品设计方向转型，把这种系统性思维直接落到地理空间界面、决策辅助工具和人机协作流程上。
